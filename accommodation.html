Travel Tips

Before You Book

Practical advice for choosing accommodation in Lesotho — a country of mountain roads and dramatic seasons.

Check Road Access

Some mountain lodges are reached via gravel or 4x4-only roads. Confirm with the property how best to reach them and whether a standard car is suitable.

Consider the Season

Winter brings snow and cold nights at altitude — confirm heating and road conditions. Summer can bring afternoon storms. Pack accordingly.

Plan Transport

Public transport exists but is limited in remote areas. Ask your accommodation about transfers, shuttles, or recommended local drivers.

Check Amenities

Power and Wi-Fi can be limited in remote areas. Ask about water, heating, meals, and mobile signal before you commit — especially in winter.

Contact Directly

Confirm the booking, price, and payment method with the accommodation provider directly. Never rely only on third-party listings.

Bring Cash

ATMs are widely available in Maseru and larger towns, but rural accommodation may only accept cash. Carry enough Maloti or Rand for the trip.
