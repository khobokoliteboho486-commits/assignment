The Mountain Kingdom

A Nation Above the Clouds

Lesotho is the only country in the world entirely above 1,000 metres in elevation. Known as the "Kingdom in the Sky," this landlocked nation is cradled by the Maluti Mountains and the Drakensberg escarpment.

Beyond its dramatic landscapes, Lesotho is home to the Basotho people — a nation with a proud heritage, distinctive traditions, and a deep connection to the mountains that shelter them.

  • Highest lowest point of any country on Earth
  • Rich Basotho culture and living heritage
  • UNESCO World Heritage and protected wilderness

Things to Do

Unforgettable Experiences

From high-altitude skiing to pony trekking through mountain villages — Lesotho rewards every kind of traveller.

Pony Trekking

Explore mountain trails the traditional Basotho way, guided by local horsemen through remote villages.

Hiking & Climbing

Trek to Thabana Ntlenyana, Southern Africa's highest peak, or follow trails through the Maluti highlands.

Skiing & Snow

Afriski Mountain Resort offers skiing and snowboarding at 3,222 metres during the winter months.

Culture & Heritage

Visit Thaba Bosiu, Morija, and living villages to experience Basotho traditions, crafts, and cuisine.

Why Lesotho

A Destination Like No Other

01

Unrivalled Altitude

Every inch of Lesotho lies above 1,000 metres. The air is clear, the stars are brilliant, and the views stretch for hundreds of kilometres.

02

Living Culture

The Basotho people welcome visitors with genuine warmth. Experience traditional music, blanket-wearing, and mountain village life.

03

Four-Season Adventure

Summer hiking, winter skiing, spring wildflowers, and autumn clarity — Lesotho offers a different face in every season.
